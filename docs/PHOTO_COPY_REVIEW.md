# Rochester customer and service-photo review

Date: September 10, 2026
Scope: Photo selection, non-generative clarity adjustments and customer-facing copy. Keep the established visual design and Color Studio intact.

## Editorial assessment

The current design is polished and approachable. Its service copy should answer practical questions more directly: what can be painted, whether a one-room project is welcome, who the customer talks to, what location information to provide, and what happens after an estimate request.

This is an editorial review, not a Rochester customer survey or a measured conversion-rate result. Public Rochester painting-service pages were used only to check common presentation patterns, such as concrete service names, preparation, direct contact and free estimates. No competitor's credentials, prices, reviews, service boundaries or guarantees were adopted.

## Changes

- Preserve the emotional hero headline, typography, colors, spacing, navigation, cards, mobile actions, process and Color Studio.
- Make painting and the Rochester, NY area explicit in the hero eyebrow and search/social metadata.
- Replace ambiguous phrases such as "scoped separately" and "targeted refresh" with specific rooms, ceilings, doors, trim and preparation work.
- Welcome a single-room or several-room project without suggesting that professional care is reserved for whole-house work.
- Clarify direct contact with Justin, request the customer's town or ZIP code and preferred timing, and explain that an estimate request does not reserve a painting date.
- Use consistent Rochester-area language. Do not invent a list of covered towns or promise county-wide service.
- Do not add insurance, licensing, lead-safe certification, warranties, ratings, availability, response-time promises or pricing without owner verification.

## Photo selection

It is explicitly labeled "Garage example · Not a Cimo project" in the image caption and identified as illustrative in the section note. 4. Small-business painting: the close view of masked workshop metal shelving from Justin's folder. It illustrates the metal-surface preparation work discussed in the September 6 meeting, not a claim that Cimo painted an entire commercial facility.

The portrait blue industrial-panel photos and loose brown workshop panel were considered but not chosen. The shelving photograph makes the preparation detail easier to understand.

## Image integrity

Project-photo edits are limited to orientation, deliberate cropping, downsampling, modest global brightness/contrast and restrained sharpening. No generative fill, object removal, repainting, smoothing away defects, invented finishes or fabricated before/after pairs. Raw source photos are not shipped. Public derivatives contain no source EXIF/GPS metadata.

Source filenames and hashes, crop coordinates and transformation parameters are retained in `project-photo-sources.json`. The garage example's photographer, license, source page and crop are recorded separately in `garage-photo-source.json`.
