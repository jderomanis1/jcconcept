A better preview in three steps

1. Start simple. Use a clear, well-lit photo. Tap a flat area with Select. Color selection is an aid, not automatic object recognition.

2. Refine the edges. Similar-colored floors or furniture can confuse Select. Use Outline to tap around the area, then Apply outline. Outline a cutout protects a window or object. Erase cleans up small spots.

3. Add another color. Choose Add surface before selecting a different wall. Each surface keeps its own color. Move lets you pan and pinch-zoom; Fit brings the whole image back.

Keyboard: focus the image, use arrow keys to move the cursor, Enter to select or place an outline point, and Escape to cancel an outline. Undo: Ctrl/⌘ Z. Redo: Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z. Template surfaces and colors also work with Tab and Enter.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and phone formats your browser can open. Maximum 20 MB. If a HEIC photo will not open, export it as JPEG.

The Cimo Standard

Great results start
before the paint.

Every home and surface is different. Preparation is tailored to the project, not skipped to get to the color.

Here is what goes into a full interior painting project.

Protect. Prepare. Paint. Review.Care for your space, from first step to last.
Let’s talk about your project ↗
01Prepare & protect your space

Dust and clean the work area so protective tape can adhere. Cover furniture with protective plastic, and protect floors, baseboards and surrounding surfaces before sanding and preparation begin.

02Repair & prepare surfaces

Inspect for holes, cracks, dents and other imperfections. Complete the necessary repairs to create a smooth foundation for painting.

03Sand & clean

Sand repaired and rough areas smooth. Remove remaining dust and debris so the surface is ready for the next step.

04Prime when needed

Prime bare surfaces, repairs, stains or problem areas when necessary to support an even, durable finish.

05Apply the paint carefully

Use the tools and techniques suited to the surface to achieve smooth, consistent coverage.

06Final coats & details

Apply additional coats as needed, then complete detailed touch-ups for a clean, uniform appearance.

07Final inspection & cleanup

Inspect the work, address finishing details and clean the area. Leave the space refreshed and ready to enjoy.
