The Cimo Standard

A thoughtful path
to a professional finish.

This is how Cimo approaches customer service and painting work: a clear, practical process shaped around the agreed project scope.

  1. 01

    Clear Scope

    Understand what is being painted and what the customer wants before work begins.

  2. 02

    Proper Prep

    Prepare surfaces appropriately through sanding, minor repair and priming when needed.

  3. 03

    Careful Finish

    Paint deliberately with attention to edges, coverage and finish quality.

  4. 04

    Respect the Space

    Maintain clean work areas and perform thorough cleanup.

  5. 05

    Final Review

    Review the finished work and confirm the agreed scope has been addressed.
