# Cimo Website Concept Feedback

- Does this feel like Cimo?
- Does it feel professional enough?
- Does it feel too expensive, too cheap, or about right?
- What feels most like your business?
- What feels least like your business?

## Homepage

- Can you immediately tell what Cimo does?
- Is the main message accurate?
- Would you change the headline?
- Is anything missing?

## Services

- Are the right services emphasized?
- Are any services missing?
- Should anything be removed?

## Images

- Does this photography style feel right?
- What real project photos do we have that could replace these?

## Estimate Experience

- Does the proposed estimate flow make sense?
- What information do you actually need from a customer before speaking with them?

## Mobile

- Does this feel easy to use on your phone?

## Final Question

If a potential customer saw this website, would you be proud to have it represent Cimo Home Refreshments?
